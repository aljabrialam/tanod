# Spec 006: Daily briefing

## Requirements
- FR-006-01: A briefing page grouped as Needs you, Stopped, You should know, Routine (a count).
- FR-006-02: Every line opens its evidence.
- FR-006-03: A scheduled task turns the day's history into a narrated video of 60 seconds or less, in plain language, generated in QwenWork.
- FR-006-04: No rule codes or jargon in the briefing.

## Acceptance
- A non-technical reader can explain every line of the briefing back.
