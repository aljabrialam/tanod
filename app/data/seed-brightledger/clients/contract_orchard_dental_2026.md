SERVICES AGREEMENT dated 6 Jan 2026 between Brightledger and Orchard Dental Clinic Pte Ltd.
1. Scope: monthly bookkeeping and payroll for 9 staff. Fee SGD 2,400 per month.
2. Clinic patient records are never part of the bookkeeping scope.
3. Personal data received in error must be deleted and reported to the client within 24 hours.
4. Either party may end this agreement with 30 days written notice.
