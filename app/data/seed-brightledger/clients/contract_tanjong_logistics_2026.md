SERVICES AGREEMENT dated 12 Jan 2026 between Brightledger and Tanjong Logistics Pte Ltd.
1. Scope: monthly bookkeeping and quarterly GST review. Fee SGD 2,400 per month.
2. A late-filing surcharge of SGD 600 applies only when the delay is caused by Brightledger. No surcharge applies when the client delivers records late.
3. Records are due from the client by the 3rd of each month.
4. Either party may end this agreement with 30 days written notice.
